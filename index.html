About

I am a {{ site.position | downcase }} in Eric Toberer's group at the {{ site.institution }}, within the NSF Institute for Data-Driven Dynamical Design (ID4). I earned my PhD in Materials Science at Mines in 2025 with Diego Gómez-Gualdrón, combining free-energy calculations and machine learning to ask which predicted metal–organic frameworks can actually exist.

Before that, I was an experimentalist. I earned a PhD in Engineering at Universidad de los Andes in Colombia, studying molecules at fluid–fluid interfaces, after training as a chemical engineer at Universidad Industrial de Santander. That path, from the lab bench to the computer, shapes how I build models: they should answer questions experimentalists actually have.
